Bespoke Tailoring

The art of bespoke tailoring begins with understanding you. Unlike made-to-measure, bespoke means your garment is built from a blank slate — no standard patterns, no shortcuts.

Every seam is individually placed, every dart is calculated for your unique posture, and every detail is your choice — fabric, lining, buttons, embellishments.

The Bespoke Process

1
Style Consultation (60 min)
Review inspiration references, discuss occasion, lifestyle, fabric preferences and design goals with your dedicated consultant.
2
20-Point Measurement Session
Our senior tailor takes comprehensive measurements covering bust, waist, hip, shoulder slope, armscye, back width and 14 more critical points.
3
Pattern Drafting & Toile
A personal pattern block is drafted. A test garment (toile) in muslin is fitted first to identify any adjustments before cutting your chosen fabric.
4
Primary Cutting & Stitching
The actual fabric is cut and hand-stitched by the same master tailor throughout, ensuring consistent craftsmanship.
5
Fitting Sessions (2–3)
You try the garment at each stage, and adjustments are made in real-time to achieve a flawless fit.
6
Final Finishing & Delivery
Hand-finishing, pressing, steaming and packaging. Your garment is delivered in our signature box with a garment care card.
Timeline: 10–21 days
Starting from: ₹2,500
Fittings: 2–3 included

Bridal Tip from Our Team

Book your bridal appointment at least 3–4 months before your wedding. Heavily embroidered pieces with zardozi or cutwork require 6–8 weeks of handcraft time alone.

How To Measure

Our Measurement Process

Perfect fit starts with perfect measurements. Our 20-point system captures every nuance of your body to produce a garment that feels like it was grown for you.

Chest / Bust
Full & high bust measurement
Waist
Natural waist at narrowest point
Hip
Full hip at widest point
Length
Shoulder to hem & inseam
Shoulder Width
Across the back shoulder line
Arm & Sleeve
Upper arm, wrist & full length
Back Length
Nape of neck to waist
Thigh & Knee
For trousers & lower garments
