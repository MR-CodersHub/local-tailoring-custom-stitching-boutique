Common Questions

Before You Visit

Yes — there is a paid parking facility directly opposite our studio on Atelier Lane. Street parking is available within a 2-minute walk on Hill Road. We recommend arriving 10 minutes early.
Walk-ins are welcome for fabric browsing and general enquiries. However, for fittings and consultations, an appointment is strongly recommended to ensure your dedicated tailor is available and the private suite is prepared for you.
A standard consultation takes 45–60 minutes. Bridal consultations can take 90 minutes to 2 hours as we discuss the full wedding wardrobe. Please plan accordingly and don't rush the experience!
Bring your inspiration images (phone or printouts), any fabric swatches you may have, and the footwear you plan to wear with the garment. For bridal consultations, bring your jewellery photos and any existing pieces you want to coordinate with.
