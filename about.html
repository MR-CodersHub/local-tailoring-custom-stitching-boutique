Our Story of Craft

18 years of stitching dreams into reality — one garment, one client, one story at a time.

Our Founding Story

Born from a Passion for Perfection

In 2008, master tailor Priya Nair opened a small studio in Bandra with a single sewing machine, a collection of vintage patterns, and an unwavering belief that every person deserves clothing that celebrates their uniqueness.

Word spread quickly. What began as a neighbourhood boutique became Mumbai's most celebrated tailoring atelier — trusted by brides, professionals, celebrities and everyday clients who refuse to settle for ready-made.

Today, Maison Élite employs 25 master craftspeople, uses fabrics sourced from Varanasi, Surat, Kanchipuram and beyond — yet the philosophy remains unchanged: every garment is personal.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Every decision we make — from thread selection to final stitching — is guided by these foundational principles.

Precision Craftsmanship

We never rush perfection. Every seam is measured twice, every buttonhole is hand-finished, every hem is perfectly levelled.

Premium Materials

We source only the finest silks, brocades, georgettes and embroidered fabrics from India's most respected textile mills.

Client Partnership

You are not a transaction — you are a collaborator. We listen deeply, understand intimately and create accordingly.

Ethical Practice

Fair wages, safe conditions and sustainable sourcing — our artisans are the heart of our business and we treat them accordingly.

Timely Excellence

We respect your timeline. Every order is delivered as promised — because your occasions cannot wait.

Lifetime Relationships

Our clients stay with us for years — for weddings, festivals, promotions and new chapters. We grow together.
