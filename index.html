Our Story

Where Thread Meets Soul

Since 2008, Maison Élite has been the trusted atelier for discerning clients seeking garments that transcend fashion — clothing that tells their personal story.

Founded by master tailor Priya Nair, our studio has grown from a small workshop to Mumbai's most celebrated tailoring boutique — yet our approach remains beautifully intimate.
