The Complete Bridal Outfit Planning Guide: 6 Months Before Your Wedding

Priya Nair
Founder & Master Tailor
October 10, 2026 · 8 min read · 24 comments

Planning your bridal wardrobe can feel overwhelming — but with the right timeline and expert guidance, it becomes one of the most joyful parts of your wedding journey. As someone who has helped over 200 brides dress for their biggest day, here is my definitive guide.

6 Months Before: Start Your Research

Begin by gathering inspiration — Pinterest boards, Instagram saves, bridal magazines. At this stage, you're not committing to anything; you're discovering your aesthetic. Are you drawn to traditional heavy embroidery, or do you prefer minimalist silk elegance? Once you have 20–30 reference images, patterns will emerge.

This is also the time to set your budget. Bridal outfits typically span a wide range — from ₹25,000 for a beautiful, simpler lehenga to ₹5 lakh+ for haute couture hand-embroidered masterpieces. Being clear on budget helps your tailor recommend realistic options.

Expert Tip

Book your first consultation at least 5–6 months before the wedding. For heavily embroidered pieces with zardozi, cut-work or hand kantha embroidery, our team needs 3–4 months of crafting time alone.

4 Months Before: Your First Consultation

By now, you should book your atelier appointment. Bring your inspiration images, fabric swatches if you've sourced any, and come with an open mind. A good bridal tailor will listen deeply, ask about your venue, body comfort preferences and any cultural expectations.

What Happens at Your Consultation

  • Full body measurement session (20 points for bridal garments)
  • Discussion of lehenga silhouette — A-line, circular, mermaid, fish-cut
  • Fabric selection from our premium collection — silk, velvet, net, organza
  • Embellishment choices — zardozi, thread work, mirror work, sequins
  • Colour selection and matching with jewellery
  • Timeline and fitting schedule confirmation
"The most beautiful brides I've dressed were not the ones who spent the most money — they were the ones who knew themselves. When a bride can tell me exactly how she wants to feel in her outfit, we create magic together."

— Priya Nair, Founder, Maison Élite

2 Months Before: First Fitting

Your lehenga is now cut and basted together. The first fitting is about structure — checking the fall of the skirt, the shoulder line, the blouse silhouette. You'll wear the toile (test garment) or the actual fabric in early form. At this stage, major structural adjustments are made.

Important: wear the exact blouse you plan to wear underneath, and bring your bridal footwear. Even a 1-inch heel change affects where the hem falls.

1 Month Before: Second Fitting

The garment is now nearly complete. The embroidery is attached, the blouse is finished, and the dupatta placement is tested. This fitting focuses on refinements — checking how the embroidery lies, ensuring the blouse fits precisely, and any final hem adjustments.

Final Week: Last Fitting & Delivery

The final fitting happens 5–7 days before the wedding. All finishing touches are done — final pressing, steaming, and packaging. Your outfit is delivered in our signature ivory box with a care card detailing how to preserve the garment for years.

Don't Forget These Extras

  1. Pre-stitch your saree fall and petticoat separately
  2. Have your dupatta's border finished with matching thread
  3. Get a matching potli or clutch stitched from leftover fabric
  4. Consider a backup blouse in a complementary fabric
